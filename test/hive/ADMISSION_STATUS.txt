HIVE ADMISSION STATUS -- 2026-11-05

R5 integration is qualified for mainline admission. R5_ADMISSION.txt records
the native-probe, root/standalone test, platform and header-only consumer gates.
CORE_PREPARATION.txt and SKIPFIELD_PREPARATION.txt remain historical evidence
for the earlier representations and their separate acceptance decisions.

Allocator-policy admission is covered by specification section 4.6 and the
accepted R2/R3 review: allocator rebind, retained fancy allocation pointers,
construction rollback and allocation-limit clamping.

This is not a claim of complete conformance on every toolchain or ABI. Sort
exception handling is a project decision: promise basic validity, unchanged
size and no leaks, with element order and individual values unspecified after
an exception. This is not a claim that the standard has settled value retention;
LWG 4584 concerns construction preconditions, not exception guarantees. Keep
the current implementation and its stronger comparator-exception observations
until a separately reviewed change. Existing wide-counter exhaustion remains
outside this admission. Iterator comparisons remain consistent with traversal;
reused slots traverse in physical address order within each active block.
No global logical-chain experiment or shared <version> wrapper is adopted.

The owner accepted R2/R3 after independent review. The original noisy timing
batches remain inconclusive; the later controlled review and revised B3 are
recorded in SKIPFIELD_PREPARATION.txt. get_iterator has an explicit flat-address
support boundary. R2.1 iteration optimization and R4 sort acceleration remain
deferred; R5 does not change the reviewed container algorithms or representation.

The opt-in standalone harness records the supported validation surface. A
<version>-only backport feature-macro test is deliberately deferred, not passed.
Ordinary downstream header-only consumers gain no GoogleTest dependency.
