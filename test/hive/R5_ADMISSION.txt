HIVE R5 INTEGRATION ADMISSION -- 2026-11-05

Disposition
-----------
The reviewed skipfield implementation is qualified for mainline integration.
R5 adds wrapper/probe, build/test, consumer, example and documentation support;
it does not change container algorithms, representation or performance policy.
R2.1 iteration optimization and R4 sort acceleration remain deferred.

The owner accepted the independent R2/R3 review and replaced B3 with dense
traversal <= 1.25 times plf::hive, measured externally by the reviewer. The
reported 1.17 passes. Earlier list-relative failures and noisy measurements
remain in SKIPFIELD_PREPARATION.txt; R5 did not rerun or replace those data.
No third-party implementation or paper pseudocode was imported.

Original work/hive-core-20261104, work/hive-skipfield-20261105 and the
hive-core-linked-baseline-20261104 tag remain unchanged. Selected preparation
history was replayed onto mainline, with identical final source trees before
R5 edits. Two attribution-format corrections affect only new commit copies,
not published history. Original provenance is retained in ADMISSION_STATUS.txt.

Integration Surface
-------------------
- <hive> follows the three-state CMake policy at the selected language mode:
  complete native stands aside, partial native warns and stands aside, and
  no native declaration injects the backport. FORCE is diagnostic-only.
- Full probes instantiate operations in function bodies. Independent partial
  roots cover the macro, limits, template and PMR surfaces. Modular probe
  contents participate in fingerprint invalidation; native MSVC hive headers
  are optional. No shared <version> shadow header was introduced.
- FORGE_TEST_ENABLE_HIVE controls the root test group. Root tests use the real
  forge::std target; the standalone harness retains its isolated overlay.
  Both retain the reviewed runtime, compile and independent-model assertions.
- Native fixtures check public-target consumption and actual main test-tree
  registration, including warm-cache changes. Synthetic COMPLETE proves the
  selection mechanism, not the quality of a real native implementation.
- Source include/add_subdirectory and installed find_package consumers use
  both forge::std and forge::forge. Explicit ENABLED/PARTIAL manifests prevent
  a missing executable from silently passing the consumer verifier.
- The stable-handle example, three READMEs and focused documentation are
  registered and consistent. The old version-wrapper comment is removed.
  Default block limits are documented for all element types, subject to clamps.

Header-Only Consumer Checks
---------------------------
Source archives used for consumer checks deliberately omit GoogleTest and
confirm that it remains absent after configuration. Include/add_subdirectory
and installed consumers passed on Linux Clang C++26 and GCC C++23, AppleClang
C++23/C++26 and Windows MSVC. Both public targets are exercised, together with
existing export/collision and optional Threads checks. No dependency download
or GoogleTest linkage is required by those consumers.

A separate install from the tests-enabled LLVM build contains no GoogleTest
artifacts or references. The standalone Hive harness installs nothing.
Including <version> before <hive> is covered; a <version>-only feature-macro
promise remains deliberately deferred.

Review And Remaining Boundaries
------------------------------
Independent integration review identified three verification gaps: silent
consumer executable skipping, missing real test-registration assertions in
native fixtures, and an insufficient sanitizer registration count. All three
were corrected and accepted on re-review, then exercised by the parent runs.
Initial harness failures were fixed and the affected full/focused runs repeated;
raw logs are retained, not replaced by a success-only account.

The flat-address get_iterator support boundary, sort exception policy and
existing wide-counter exhaustion limitation remain as documented. This is a
bounded integration admission, not universal conformance or performance proof.
The original cost runner now accepts an optional CPU-affinity selection and
records it in its protocol; this change does not alter old measurements.

Taskbook, history map, independent-review notes, platform commands and raw logs
are local ignored evidence under build/review/hive-integration-20261105/.
